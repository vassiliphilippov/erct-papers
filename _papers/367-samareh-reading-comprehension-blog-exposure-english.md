abstract: >-
  The present study aims to provide experimental evidence on
  exploring the blogs to develop Iranian EFL learners' reading
  comprehension. To this end, forty intermediate female
  learners after taking the placement test were randomly
  assigned to control and experimental group. After twenty two
  sessions of blog exposure, a comparison of the reading test
  scores of the control and experimental groups revealed that
  EFL learners favor surfing the blogs which can act as an
  effective strategy of improving their reading comprehension.
