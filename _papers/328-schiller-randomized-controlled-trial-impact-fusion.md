title: >
  A Randomized Controlled Trial of the Impact of the Fusion
  Reading Intervention on Reading Achievement and Motivation
  for Adolescent Struggling Readers
authors: >
  Ellen Schiller, Xin Wei, Sara Thayer, Jose Blackorby,
  Harold Javitz, and Cyndi Williamson
paper_link: "https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED535544"
abstract: >-
  This study estimates the effect of one year of Fusion
  Reading implementation, a multistrategy supplemental
  reading intervention for struggling adolescent readers in
  grades 6-10. The authors conducted a randomized controlled
  trial in seven Michigan middle and high schools. Students
  in the intervention condition received Fusion Reading as a
  supplemental reading class, while control students engaged
  in nonliteracy, "business-as-usual" activities. After one
  year of a two-year intervention, Fusion students showed
  significantly higher sight word efficiency and sentence
  comprehension than control students; other reading and
  motivation outcomes were not significant.
