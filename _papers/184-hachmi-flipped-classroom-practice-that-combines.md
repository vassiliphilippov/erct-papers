title: >
  A flipped classroom practice that combines gamification and
  analogy to improve learning outcomes for online students
authors: A Hachmi
paper_link: "https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1361-6552/ae5568"
abstract: >-
  This study aims to improve the results of online learning of a
  simple electrical circuit. It provides a solution to the
  barriers of synchronous distance learning, particularly in
  terms of interactivity and reducing the role of the teacher.
  The proposed solution is to adopt a flipped classroom practice
  that combines gamification and analogy (FCPCGA). The
  mixed-methods methodology includes quantitative and qualitative
  measures using multiple-choice questionnaires to assess
  academic performance and collect feedback on the FCPCGA. It
  uses a control group and an experimental group and measures
  learning outcomes before and after the intervention. The
  results showed an improvement in learning outcomes for students
  in the experimental group who expressed satisfaction with the
  FCPCGA.
