abstract: >-
  The "conceptual metaphor" fundamentally shapes human cognition to a
  significant extent. This paper explores the connotations, evolution,
  and distinctive features of different types of conceptual metaphor
  theory. It proposes the integration of metaphor theory into college
  English vocabulary teaching to stimulate students' learning of
  vocabulary and phrases through the cognitive functions of metaphor.
  Through comparative analysis, the paper reveals the potential
  application of conceptual metaphor in college English vocabulary
  teaching and its impact on learning outcomes.
